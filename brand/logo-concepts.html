Supermetry logo concepts

Three directions for the mark. Each is shown large, as a lockup with the name on light and dark grounds, and at favicon sizes, where most people will actually see it. The amber accent stands for a live visitor in all three.

Before you commit: Supermetrics (with an s) is an established marketing-analytics company. Run a trademark search on “Supermetry” before you spend money on the brand.

B. Tally

Four rising strokes crossed by a fifth, like counting marks on a wall. It says “we count visits, not people”, which is the privacy promise. The cross stroke reads as a trend line. Busier at small sizes.

C. Path

A single-stroke s that traces a visitor’s path through a site, ending on a live dot. The friendliest of the three, but monogram s marks are common, so it is the least ownable.
