Supermetry logo concepts

Round two, in the direction of your example: a heavy geometric wordmark, a mark built from thick straight strokes, white on electric blue. Each concept is shown as a hero lockup, on light and dark, and at favicon sizes.

Before you commit: Supermetrics (with an s) is an established marketing-analytics company. Run a trademark search on “Supermetry” before you spend money on the brand.

Gauge

A measuring dial with the needle pushed past the top: the reading is going up. “-metry” means measuring, and the needle points up and to the right, which is the “super” part. Built from five straight strokes, so it holds up at 16 px.

Exponent

Three rising bars with a short stroke raised off the top right, like the 2 in x². Bars mean analytics; the raised stroke means “super”. The most literal of the three.
