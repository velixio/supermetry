# Code of Conduct

Supermetry is built in public by people with different backgrounds and experience. We want it to be a place where anyone can ask questions, report problems and contribute without being harassed or belittled.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say what you would change.
- Accept feedback gracefully. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public, so others can learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about someone's race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, appearance or background.
- Sexualized language or images, unwelcome attention or stalking.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, spam, or promoting unrelated products.
- Pasting personal data of real visitors into issues or pull requests. Remove it from logs, exports and screenshots first.

## Where it applies

In all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel where someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem by email to **[CONDUCT CONTACT EMAIL]**. Reports are read only by the maintainers and handled in confidence. Do not use public issues for conduct reports. For a security problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What maintainers do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, with an explanation when it is safe to give one. A maintainer who has a conflict of interest in a report steps aside from it. Maintainers who do not follow this code can be removed by the other maintainers or by Yupcha, which stewards the project.

## Changes

This code may be updated. Changes are made through a pull request like any other change.
